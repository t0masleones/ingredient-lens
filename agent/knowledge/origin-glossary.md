# Origin glossary: animal or plant? (EN / PT) - v0.1

Aid for classifying ingredients when the profile is a diet (vegan, vegetarian). It is not exhaustive. If the label says something more specific (for example "soy lecithin" or "vegetable oil"), use the label.

## Definitions used here
- **Vegan:** no animal-derived ingredients at all, including dairy, eggs, honey, gelatin, and insect-derived colors and glazes.
- **Vegetarian (lacto-ovo, default):** no meat, poultry, fish or slaughter by-products (gelatin, lard, tallow, animal rennet). Dairy, eggs and honey are allowed. Practices vary: if it matters (for example carmine, or cheese made with animal rennet), ask the user for their rule.

## Animal origin (certain)
- Meat and poultry: beef, pork, chicken, turkey, bacon, ham, sausage (carne, boi, porco, frango, peru, bacon, presunto, linguiça)
- Stock, broth or bouillon made from meat or bone (caldo de carne, de galinha, de osso)
- Animal fats: lard, tallow, suet, schmaltz, dripping (banha, sebo, gordura animal)
- Fish and seafood: fish, anchovy, fish sauce, oyster sauce, fish oil, cod liver oil, krill oil (peixe, anchova, molho de peixe, molho de ostra, óleo de peixe)
- Dairy: milk, butter, cream, cheese, yogurt, whey, casein, ghee (leite, manteiga, creme, queijo, iogurte, soro de leite, caseína)
- Egg: whole egg, egg white, egg yolk, albumen (ovo, clara, gema, albumina)
- Bee products: honey, beeswax E901, royal jelly, propolis (mel, cera de abelha, geleia real, própolis)
- Gelatin and collagen (gelatina, colágeno). Gelatin has no E or INS number: it is a food, not an additive. E441 / INS 441 is superglycerinated hydrogenated rapeseed oil (plant).
- Carmine, cochineal, E120, natural red 4 (carmim, cochonilha): insect-derived
- Shellac, confectioner's glaze, E904 (goma-laca): insect secretion
- Lanolin (from sheep wool)
- Edible bone phosphate E542

## Can be either (never guess: ask the supplier or check the product label)
- Oil, fat or shortening with no stated source (óleo, gordura, gordura hidrogenada). "Vegetable oil" or "palm oil" names a plant source; "shortening" or "fat" alone does not.
- Mono- and diglycerides E471 and related emulsifiers
- Glycerin or glycerol E422 (glicerina)
- Stearic acid E570 and magnesium stearate E572 (ácido esteárico, estearato de magnésio)
- Natural flavors and flavorings (aromas naturais), including "butter flavor" or "meat flavor"
- Lecithin E322: soy or sunflower (plant) versus egg (animal). Check the stated source.
- Disodium inosinate E631, disodium guanylate E627, E635: from meat or fish, or from fermentation
- L-cysteine E920: animal-derived or fermentation
- Enzymes, rennet, lipase, pepsin (enzimas, coalho): animal, microbial or plant. Traditional hard cheeses often use animal rennet.
- Vitamin D3: sheep lanolin (animal) or lichen (plant). Omega-3 DHA and EPA: fish or algae.
- Sugar: some refiners filter cane sugar with bone char. This is a processing aid and is invisible on the label.
- Wine, beer and some juices: fining agents such as isinglass (fish) or egg or milk proteins may be used and are not listed.
- Bouillon cubes and broth powders labeled "vegetable": check the full ingredient list for animal fat or flavors.

## Plant origin (certain)
- Vegetables, fruits, grains, flours, legumes, nuts, seeds, spices, herbs (hortaliças, frutas, cereais, farinhas, leguminosas)
- Vegetable oils named by source: soy, canola, sunflower, olive, palm, coconut oil
- Soy lecithin, sunflower lecithin
- Cocoa butter, shea butter
- Coconut milk; oat, soy, almond and rice drinks
- Pectin (fruit), agar and carrageenan (seaweed), guar gum and locust bean gum (seeds)
- Annatto, beet red, turmeric, paprika extract
- Vinegar, maple syrup, agave, molasses, cane sugar (see the sugar note above)
- Tofu, tempeh, seitan, textured vegetable protein

## Mineral, synthetic or microbial (usually fine for vegan and vegetarian)
- Salt, baking soda, baking powder, calcium carbonate, iron and other minerals, water
- Citric acid, lactic acid and lactates (fermentation, not dairy), ascorbic acid
- Xanthan gum and gellan gum (microbial fermentation)
- Baker's yeast, nutritional yeast, yeast extract
- Vitamin B12 (bacterial fermentation)

## Allergen statement shortcuts
If the allergen statement lists milk, egg, fish, crustaceans or molluscs, animal-derived ingredients are present: not vegan, and fish and crustaceans are not vegetarian either. Wheat, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, celery, mustard and lupin are plant-based.

## Questions to ask a supplier (for each "Can be either")
1. What is the source of this ingredient: plant, animal or microbial?
2. Are any animal-derived processing aids used to make it?
3. Do you have a vegan or vegetarian certificate or a written declaration?
4. Is the production line shared with animal-derived products?
