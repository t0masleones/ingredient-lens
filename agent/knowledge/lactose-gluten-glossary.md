# Lactose and gluten glossary (PT / EN) - v0.1

Simplified aid for classification. It is not exhaustive. If the label says something more specific, use the label.

## Lactose and milk

### 🔴 Milk or lactose present
- leite (integral, desnatado, em pó, condensado, fermentado) / milk (whole, skim, powder, condensed)
- lactose
- soro de leite, soro de leite em pó / whey, whey powder
- creme de leite, nata / cream
- iogurte, coalhada, kefir / yogurt, kefir
- queijo, requeijão / cheese, cream cheese
- leitelho / buttermilk
- doce de leite
- composto lácteo, bebida láctea / milk compound, milk drink
- sólidos de leite, sólidos lácteos / milk solids
- chocolate ao leite / milk chocolate

### 🟡 Milk-derived, lactose amount unclear
INFORMATIVE mode: say tolerance varies. STRICT mode: treat as 🔴.
- manteiga, manteiga clarificada, ghee / butter, clarified butter (little lactose)
- queijos curados / aged cheese (little lactose)
- caseína, caseinato / casein, caseinate
- proteína do leite, proteína do soro de leite / milk protein, whey protein (isolates have less lactose than concentrates)
- lactoalbumina, lactoglobulina / lactalbumin, lactoglobulin

### 🟡 Vague terms (check the allergen statement)
- aroma natural / natural flavor
- margarina / margarine
- chocolate that is not dark
- caldos, embutidos, presunto / broths, cold cuts (milk proteins or lactose are sometimes added)

### 🟢 NOT dairy (false alarms)
- manteiga de cacau / cocoa butter
- leite de coco / coconut milk
- bebidas vegetais de arroz, aveia, soja, amêndoas / rice, oat, soy, almond drinks (but oats are a gluten source in Brazil, and almond is a tree nut)
- ácido lático, lactato (de cálcio, de sódio) / lactic acid, calcium or sodium lactate
- estearoil lactilato / stearoyl lactylate
- lactase (the enzyme that digests lactose, used in lactose-free products)
- creme de tártaro / cream of tartar

### Lactose-free claims
In Brazil, "zero lactose", "sem lactose" and similar claims mean at most 0.1 g of lactose per 100 g or mL (RDC 135/2017). That works for lactose intolerance. It does NOT work for milk allergy: the milk protein remains.

## Gluten

### 🔴 Gluten present
- trigo / wheat (farinha de trigo, wheat flour, sêmola, semolina, durum, farinha integral, cuscuz de trigo, bulgur, seitan, glúten de trigo)
- centeio / rye
- cevada / barley
- aveia / oats (treated as a gluten source in Brazil; often cross-contaminated)
- triticale
- espelta / spelt
- kamut / khorasan wheat
- malte, extrato de malte, xarope de malte / malt, malt extract, malt syrup (barley)
- cerveja / beer
- vinagre de malte / malt vinegar
- molho shoyu / soy sauce (usually contains wheat)

### 🟡 Unclear (check the gluten statement)
- amido, amido modificado / starch, modified starch
- maltodextrina / maltodextrin (usually corn)
- proteína vegetal hidrolisada / hydrolyzed vegetable protein
- extrato de levedura, levedura de cerveja / yeast extract, brewer's yeast
- aroma natural / natural flavor
- glúten de milho / corn gluten (not wheat gluten; confirm)

### 🟢 Not gluten (false alarms)
- arroz, milho, fubá, mandioca, polvilho, tapioca, batata, quinoa, amaranto, sorgo / rice, corn, cassava, tapioca, potato, quinoa, amaranth, sorghum
- trigo-sarraceno / buckwheat (not wheat, but cross-contact is common)
- soja / soy

## Cross-contact warnings
"Pode conter..." / "May contain..." means possible traces. INFORMATIVE mode: 🟡. STRICT mode: 🔴.
