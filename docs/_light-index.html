AI agent · Business analysis case study

Label compliance, first pass. Measured like a regulated product.

Ingredient Lens reads a food label, checks it against its own statements and the rules of Brazil, the US and the EU, and hands anything doubtful to a person. The model reads and explains; a tested rules engine decides.

What it does

Built around the analyst, safe for everyone

Analyst first pass

A checklist per market with the legal basis of each check, then one of three decisions: approve, request evidence or escalate with a handoff note.

Safety in code

Symptoms described now get emergency guidance only. A product is never called “safe”. Allergy and celiac profiles turn every doubt red.

Governed data and evidence

Every ingredient row cites its source. Screening is never presented as certification or as a lab result; each claim names the evidence that settles it.
