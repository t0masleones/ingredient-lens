Decisions after the cross-check (2026-10-16)#

  • Conflicts kept as curated: maionese / mayonnaise. Open Food Facts says "maybe vegan" because vegan mayonnaise exists. Traditional mayonnaise is made with egg, and vegan versions say so on the label (they match maionese vegana / vegan mayonnaise), so the curated egg flag stays.
  • Regulatory source added to 4 "not found" rows: castanha de caju and castanha do para (RDC 26/2015 annex), gergelim (FDA, FASTER Act 2021), all purpose flour (FDA, FALCPA 2004, wheat).
  • Still without a source (15 rows): the vegan analogues (vegan butter, queijo vegano...), cashew cheese, queijo de castanha, cultura lactica, fermento lacteo, lactic culture, cravo, tamarindo. They need a person to cite a source.
  • Importer bugs found by this check and fixed: peanuts were tagged as tree nuts (Open Food Facts files peanuts under nuts) and molluscs as crustaceans (both under "shellfish").
